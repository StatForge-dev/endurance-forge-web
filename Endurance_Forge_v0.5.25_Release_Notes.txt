Endurance Forge v0.5.25

Heat-map palette refinement

- Replaces the v0.5.23 heat-map colors with a substantially lighter all-blue palette.
- Below-baseline cells now use light cyan-blue shades.
- Above-baseline cells now use light cornflower/periwinkle-blue shades.
- Near-baseline cells are also lighter while remaining visually distinct from deviations.
- Updates text contrast for readability on the lighter cell backgrounds.
- Keeps browser, fullscreen, SVG, and PNG heat-map colors synchronized.
- No analysis formulas, thresholds, run classification, or metric calculations were changed.
