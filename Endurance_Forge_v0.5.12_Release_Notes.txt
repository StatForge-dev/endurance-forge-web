Endurance Forge v0.5.12
Multi-Run Analytical Dashboard Refresh

Changes
- Replaced the large Average HR and HR Drift per-run bar-chart block with higher-value analytical views.
- Added a selectable Performance & Physiology Trend chart:
  * Pace/HRR efficiency
  * HR drift
  * Pace
  * HRR
  * Average HR
  * EF aerobic-fitness estimate
- Added earlier-vs-later median summaries when at least four usable observations exist for a run type. These remain descriptive and do not label changes as good or bad.
- Added a selectable Relationship Analysis scatter plot:
  * Pace vs HRR
  * Pace vs HR drift
  * Pace vs ground contact time
  * Pace vs cadence
  * Pace vs stride length
  * Pace vs vertical ratio
  * HRR vs Pace/HRR efficiency
- Preserved treadmill/outdoor visual separation in all multi-run charts.
- Improved sparse-series behavior: a run type now needs at least three usable observations before points are connected by a trend line. One or two observations remain individual points.
- Retained the existing selectable Running Dynamics Trend for cadence, stride length, ground contact time, vertical ratio, vertical oscillation, and running power.
- Retained the Run Pattern Heat Map and its existing baseline-direction logic.
- Updated application version to v0.5.12.

Design intent
The Multi-Run page now separates three analytical questions:
1. Trend: How are performance and physiological measures changing over time?
2. Relationship: Which measured variables move together?
3. Pattern/outlier analysis: Which runs differ from comparable baseline behavior?

Notes
- Relationship plots are exploratory and do not imply causation.
- Mixed treadmill/outdoor regression lines are intentionally not imposed.
- Higher or lower values are not automatically labeled as improvement or deterioration where physiological interpretation depends on context.
