Endurance Forge v0.5.34

Experimental Analytics — Two-Path FIP Model

- Adds two validated FIP paths: change point and progressive onset.
- Progressive onset uses a 15–30% baseline and an adaptive 2–5% loss threshold based on observed durability loss.
- Requires local deterioration, future loss, and persistence; selects the strongest supported candidate rather than the first eligible point.
- Removes dependence on the former fixed 40% onset boundary that caused clustering during validation.
- Adds progressive-onset diagnostics and method labels to single-run and multi-run views.
- Updates EF-TN-002 methodology.
- No changes to DI, PHH, ARL, or Recovery Kinetics.
