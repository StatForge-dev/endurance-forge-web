Endurance Forge v0.5.26 — Experimental Analytics

NEW
- Adds a top-level Experimental tab and /experimental/ route.
- Adds five exploratory, confidence-gated metrics:
  1. Durability Index (DI)
  2. Fatigue Inflection Point (FIP)
  3. Pace–HR Hysteresis (PHH)
  4. Aerobic Response Lag (ARL)
  5. Recovery Kinetics (RK)
- Supports FIT, TCX, and GPX activity uploads using local browser processing.
- Supports treadmill truth distance/time correction before experimental analysis.
- Reports unavailable/not detected states instead of forcing weak estimates.
- Adds clear experimental-use guidance and confidence labels.

METHOD NOTES
- DI compares speed/HR efficiency in 20–40% vs 60–80% analysis windows.
- FIP uses segmented regression and requires meaningful fit improvement plus deterioration after the breakpoint.
- PHH uses normalized speed–HR trajectory area and requires workload variation.
- ARL uses lagged correlation of workload and HR changes over 0–120 seconds.
- RK detects meaningful post-effort HR declines and estimates exponential recovery time constant tau plus HRR60.

CAUTION
These are exploratory Endurance Forge analytics, not clinical measures, universal performance scores, or device-manufacturer metrics. Thresholds and algorithms may evolve during validation.
