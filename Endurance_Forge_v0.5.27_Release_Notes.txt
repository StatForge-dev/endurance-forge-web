Endurance Forge v0.5.27 — Experimental Multi-Run Analysis

Added a Single Run / Multi-Run switch to Experimental.
Multi-Run Experimental now:
- accepts multiple FIT, TCX, and GPX activities
- calculates the five Experimental metrics independently for each qualifying activity
- preserves unavailable/not-detected results rather than converting them to zero
- provides All Runs, Treadmill, and Outdoor filters
- shows longitudinal charts for Durability Index, Fatigue Inflection Point, Pace-HR Hysteresis, Aerobic Response Lag, and Recovery Kinetics
- includes an activity-level results table and metric availability counts
- keeps raw activity processing local in the browser

Experimental metrics remain exploratory within-person analytics and are not clinical or universal performance scores.
