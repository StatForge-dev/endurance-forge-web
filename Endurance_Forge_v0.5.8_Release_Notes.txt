Endurance Forge v0.5.8 — Heat-Map Usability and Export Update

HEAT-MAP VIEWING
- Added Fit to screen / Comfort view toggle for both Multi-Run heat maps.
- Added Full screen viewing for each heat map.
- Compact mode reduces cell padding and typography while preserving values and robust-deviation labels.

EXPORTS
- Added PNG, SVG, and CSV download options for the Run Pattern Heat Map.
- Added PNG, SVG, and CSV download options for the Metric Relationship Heat Map.
- Exports are generated locally in the browser.

COLOR SEMANTICS
- Replaced the previous blue/red-style diverging palette with a direction-neutral teal/amber palette.
- Cool teal = below the comparable same-type baseline.
- Neutral = near baseline.
- Warm amber = above the comparable same-type baseline.
- Colors explicitly do not mean universally good/bad.
- Pace is now intuitive: because pace is stored as time per mile, faster-than-baseline pace is lower and appears teal; slower-than-baseline pace appears amber.
- Correlation heat-map colors likewise indicate negative vs positive association, not quality.

METHODOLOGY
- Updated heat-map documentation to describe the palette and export/view controls.

UNCHANGED
- Robust median/MAD deviation calculations.
- Same-run-type baseline separation.
- Correlation calculations.
- EF estimator and evidence rules.
- Single-run analysis and treadmill correction.
