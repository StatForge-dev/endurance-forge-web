Endurance Forge v0.5.21

SIMPLER MULTI-RUN SELECTION

- One primary Runs to analyze selector controls the entire Multi-Run dashboard.
- Available choices: All Runs, Last 5, Last 10, Last 30 days, First N, Last N.
- Progress Baseline has been removed as a required concept.
- Comparison is optional and off by default.
- When enabled, users can compare the selected runs with First 5, First 10, First N, or Previous N runs.
- Similar-runs filtering remains available as an advanced optional control.
- Progress trends work without requiring a comparison.

Design goal: useful, user-friendly, simple navigation and straightforward analysis without forced configuration.
