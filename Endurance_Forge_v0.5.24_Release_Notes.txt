Endurance Forge v0.5.24

Home-page refinement
- Replaced the oversized hero headline with “Find the patterns in your running.”
- Reduced hero headline size and vertical spacing so the home page is less overpowering and more application-focused.
- Preserved the existing Endurance Forge navigation, local-first message, file-format badges, and primary actions.
