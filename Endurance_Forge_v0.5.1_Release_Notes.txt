Endurance Forge v0.5.1

Parser and activity-workflow fixes

- FIT elevation support added. Endurance Forge now reads enhanced_altitude when available and falls back to standard altitude, applying the FIT scale and offset correctly.
- FIT record power is now read when present.
- TCX Garmin RunCadence is now recognized in addition to standard Cadence.
- TCX/GPX power samples are recognized when present.
- Detected Data Capabilities now includes Power.
- Added robust outdoor/treadmill auto-detection based on sustained GPS movement rather than a single coordinate.
- Single Run Analyzer now branches by activity type:
  * Outdoor: recorded distance/time/GPS/elevation/sensor data; no treadmill truth panel or treadmill EF estimate.
  * Treadmill/indoor: treadmill distance/time/grade inputs, environment context, correction export, and treadmill EF model.
- Manual activity-type override remains available.
- Multi-Run Analysis now uses the same robust activity-type detector.

No EF estimator mathematics or evidence thresholds were changed.
