Endurance Forge v0.5.29
Experimental detection reliability update

- FIP now smooths the efficiency trace before segmented regression and uses less brittle but still confidence-gated breakpoint criteria.
- ARL now uses smoothed speed/HR signals and short-window changes, reducing sensitivity to second-to-second sensor noise.
- Recovery Kinetics now detects sustained post-peak HR declines rather than requiring a strict one-sample local maximum.
- The same revised experimental engine powers Single Run and Multi-Run Experimental analysis.
- Response Timing legend now has a protected background so chart traces do not obstruct the labels.
- Missing metrics remain missing; the update does not force a FIP, ARL, or recovery result when evidence is insufficient.
