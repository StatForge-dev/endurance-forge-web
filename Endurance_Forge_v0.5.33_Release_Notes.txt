Endurance Forge v0.5.33
FIP validation and diagnostics release

- Replaces threshold-crossing FIP fallback with continuous segmented (hinge) regression.
- Uses BIC model comparison (Delta BIC >= 6) plus slope, durability-loss, boundary, and breakpoint-stability validation.
- Progressive durability loss without a validated change point is no longer labeled FIP.
- Adds expandable single-run FIP validation diagnostics.
- Adds FIP method/diagnostic context to multi-run results, including Delta BIC.
- Updates Experimental methodology to match the validated FIP implementation.
